Franco Venter

Background

I'm a seasoned finance professional with over ten years of experience across investing and accounting advisory. I take a practical, business-focused approach to adding value to businesses. I enjoy working closely with founders, operators, and investors on the decisions that matter most for their businesses.

A defining part of my career was at a later-stage growth equity firm, where I held senior finance and investment positions. That experience gave me a rare dual perspective — understanding what strong financial management looks like from inside a business, and what disciplined investors look for from the outside. I bring both lenses to every client engagement.

I'm a Chartered Accountant (CA ANZ and CA (SA)) and I'm dedicated to helping founders and business owners gain unique insight into their business and operations. I add strategic value above financial reporting and compliance.

Fintree Advisory

I founded Fintree Advisory to give Australian businesses access to the kind of senior financial thinking that's usually reserved for much larger organisations. The work is hands-on and tailored to each client's unique needs.

At Fintree, I work with founders and business owners across CFO advisory, accounting, tax, financial modelling, and business strategy.
