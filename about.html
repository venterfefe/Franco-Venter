Franco Venter

Background

I'm a seasoned finance professional with over ten years of experience across corporate finance, growth-stage companies, and investment management. I take a practical, business-focused approach to financial leadership — one shaped by years of working closely with founders, operators, and investors on the decisions that matter most.

A defining part of my career was at a later-stage VC fund, where I held senior finance and investment positions. That experience gave me a rare dual perspective — understanding what strong financial management looks like from inside a business, and what disciplined investors look for from the outside. I bring both lenses to every client engagement.

I'm a Chartered Accountant — CA ANZ and CA (SA) — and I'm dedicated to helping founders and business owners achieve genuine financial clarity and control, not just compliance.

Fintree Advisory

I founded Fintree Advisory to give Australian businesses access to the kind of senior financial thinking that's usually reserved for much larger organisations. The work is hands-on and tailored — no generic frameworks, no unnecessary overhead.

At Fintree, I work with founders and business owners across CFO advisory, accounting, tax, financial modelling, and business strategy — helping them navigate complexity, seize opportunities, and build businesses that last.
