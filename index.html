Background

I've spent over ten years in corporate finance, working across growth-stage companies and investment management. A significant part of that was at a later-stage VC fund, where I held senior finance and investment positions — giving me a rare perspective as both an operator and an investor.

That experience shapes how I work with clients today. I understand what strong financial leadership looks like from the inside, and I know what capital markets and investors expect to see from the outside. I bring both lenses to every engagement.

I'm a Chartered Accountant — CA ANZ and CA (SA) — and I take a practical, business-focused approach to financial advice. The goal is always clarity and control, not complexity.
