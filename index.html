Background

I've spent over ten years in investing and accounting advisory, working with many growth-stage companies, fund managers and traditional businesses. A significant part of my experience was at a later-stage growth equity firm, where I held senior finance and investment positions. The unique combination of those roles have given me a deep understanding of how businesses operate and grow, while also developing a strong sense of what investors and capital markets look for in a company.

That experience shapes how I work with clients today. I understand what strong financial leadership looks like from the inside, and I know what capital markets and investors expect to see from the outside. I bring both perspectives to every engagement.

I'm a Chartered Accountant (CA ANZ and CA (SA)) and I take a practical, approach to provide value to my clients. The goal is always to deliver simple high quality service to my clients.
